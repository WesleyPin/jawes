Princess Jaja and Weswes the monkey 🐒

Chapter I · The Jungle of Parissea

Once upon a time, there was a monkey named Weswes.

He lived in a vast jungle called Parissea — a place wild, hostile, and dangerous for humans. Deep in his heart, he carried a strong resentment toward them. Not without reason… humans had taken his family when he was just a baby. Since then, he had learned to survive on his own, and every single day was a fight.

One day, while searching for food to calm his hunger, he heard a scream in the distance.

A scream so powerful that it stopped him instantly.

Curious, he rushed toward the sound, swinging from vine to vine, almost falling several times in his haste. And when he finally arrived… his whole body froze.

A giant frog was about to devour a young woman.

Even with all the hatred he had for humans, he couldn't just stand there. He couldn't leave her. So without thinking, he threw himself into the fight. He fought for her. With everything he had.

Growls, blows, and bites echoed through the jungle… until, at last, the creature fled.

Chapter II · A Heart That Raced

Slowly, he turned back toward the young woman…

And suddenly, his heart began to race.
Faster than ever before.

She stood there in front of him. A princess. Beautiful beyond anything he had ever imagined. Her pink dress, slightly torn. Her messy hair. Her deep, piercing eyes. Her radiant smile.

Weswes couldn't move. She gently stepped closer. So gently… that he didn't resist. Not even for a second. Her hand softly ran through his fur.

"I am Princess Jaja. Thank you for saving me… my little monkey."

He didn't understand her words. And yet… somehow, he understood everything. Because in her eyes, something spoke louder than language.

But suddenly — voices. Footsteps. The moment shattered.

The princess's guards rushed in, pulling her away and chasing Weswes off violently. She screamed, begging them not to hurt him… But it was too late. Wounded, he had no choice but to run.

One thought echoed in his mind: He had to see her again. No matter what.

Chapter III · Two Worlds Apart

Back at the castle, the princess couldn't stop crying. He had saved her life. And in return, he had been treated like nothing. Her heart ached with every step. But what she had seen in his eyes — that kindness, that sincerity — it stayed with her. Forever.

When she returned to the kingdom of Philippea, she remained silent all day. And when night fell, she stood by her window, high in her tower. Looking toward the jungle. Thinking of him. Her little monkey.

But reality was waiting for her. Soon… she would have to marry a prince. A life chosen for her. Not by her.

Meanwhile, Weswes began a dangerous journey. To find her. The one who had changed everything. The one who had given his life meaning. He didn't know where to go. The castle was too far to be seen. But he kept moving forward. Step after step.

Along the way, he met unexpected allies — including Pablo, a gorilla who helped him survive a deadly encounter with a panther. And somehow… fate was on his side, because Pablo had escaped from the very kingdom where the princess lived.

Days passed. Long. Exhausting. Endless.

Doubt began to creep in. After all… she was a princess. And he… was just a monkey.

Chapter IV · The Tower

But just as he was about to give up — he saw it. Far in the distance.

A tower. The castle.
Massive. Imposing.
Standing proudly on the edge of a cliff.

And that tower… it didn't look like a home. It looked like a prison. But he didn't stop. One step. Then another. Until he reached it. Silently. Carefully.

That same day… it was the princess's big day. The day she would meet the man she was meant to marry. A life without love. A destiny she never chose.

A grand ball had been prepared for the occasion. She walked down the stairs in her deep pink gown. Stunning. Almost unreal. Every eye in the room was on her. But her mind… was somewhere else. Far away. Lost.

After one more forced smile… one too many…

She left. Ran to her room. And broke down. Curled up on herself. Tears falling endlessly.

Then — a noise. At her window. She froze. Slowly… she pulled the curtain aside.

And her heart stopped.
It was him.
Weswes.

Chapter V · The Fight

Her face lit up instantly. Without hesitation, she opened the window. And pulled him into her arms. Holding him tightly. As if nothing could hurt her anymore.

Time stood still. Their eyes met. And in his gaze, she saw everything — his courage, his pain, his love.

But suddenly — the door burst open. The prince entered. Sword in hand. And everything changed again.

Weswes stepped in front of her. Protecting her. Without hesitation. The fight began. Violent. Relentless. He fought with everything he had. Again. And again. But his body was getting weaker. Wound after wound.

Until — one mistake. One second. And the final blow struck.

He fell.

The princess rushed to him. Held him in her arms. But she already knew. She was losing him. So in one final moment…

She kissed him.

And suddenly — a blinding light filled the room. Everything disappeared. And when it faded… the monkey was gone.

In his place… a man. Lying in her arms. Beautiful. Unreal. But his eyes… they were still his. Weswes.

Chapter VI · Ever After

He slowly stood up. Took his staff. And faced the prince. The fight resumed. But this time… there was no doubt. Every movement was perfect. Driven by something stronger than anything else.

Love.

The prince fell. Defeated.

And when he turned toward her… she knew. It was him. Still him. Always him. Slowly, they moved closer to each other. As if the world had disappeared.

And in that moment… they kissed.

And that is how Weswes, once just a monkey, became a prince. By the side of Princess Jaja. In the kingdom of Philippea.

Together, they found happiness again. And they never let go of each other. Their love grew stronger every day. Until they became king and queen. And built a life…

Together.

✨ The End ✨
